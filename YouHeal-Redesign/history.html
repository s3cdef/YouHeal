A short history of biomagnetism

Two threads run through this story. One is laboratory science: the body produces magnetic fields that instruments can measure. The other is a therapeutic practice born in Mexico in 1988 and extended in Spain in 2014. YOU HEAL stands on the second thread and is honest about the first.
