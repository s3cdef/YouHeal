Two ways to meet Sandra

The protocol is the same in both. What changes is where your body is while the work happens: on the table in Astoria, or at home on a video call while Sandra works through the remote methods of Quantum Biomagnetism.

How an in-person session goes

Arrive a few minutes early. There is water, a quiet room, and no need to change clothes.

Walk through it in the Lab
  1. Conversation

    What brought you in, your history, and what balance would look like. About ten minutes.

  2. Scan

    You lie face-up, dressed, shoes on. Sandra tests points with a magnet and watches your legs for the shortening response. Fifteen to twenty minutes.

  3. Pairs

    Magnets are placed on each pair the scan found and left to work for fifteen to twenty-five minutes. Most people doze.

  4. Bioenergetic layer

    If you chose the specialized session, an emotional and energetic scan follows, using the practitioner as reference.

  5. Integration

    Magnets come off, you drink water, and Sandra suggests a rhythm for the next sessions.

How a remote session goes

Quantum Biomagnetism works at a distance through a witness: your name, date of birth and a recent photo stand in for your body on Sandra's table while you rest at home.

The quantum table

A working map used in Dr. Hervás's method. Sandra scans the witness over the table and places pairs on it, following the same catalog used in person.

  1. Before the call

    You send a recent photo and your date of birth. Find a quiet place to lie down with your phone or laptop nearby.

  2. Conversation

    The same ten-minute conversation, on video.

  3. Remote scan

    You rest with the camera on. Sandra works through the witness and the quantum table and tells you what she finds.

  4. Pairs hold

    Pairs are placed on the witness and held. You stay lying down; many people fall asleep here too.

  5. Integration

    A short debrief, water, and the plan for next time. A written summary arrives by email.
