Is it safe?

Sessions use static magnets on the surface of the body, over clothing. People with a pacemaker, implanted defibrillator or insulin pump, and those who are pregnant, should talk with Sandra before booking. Keep taking any medication your doctor prescribes.

Do I undress?

No. You stay fully dressed, shoes on. Wear something comfortable and empty your pockets of phones, keys and cards.

How many sessions will I need?

Many people start with a series of three, spaced one to two weeks apart, then continue as needed. Sandra will suggest a rhythm after your first scan.

Does a remote session work the same way?

The protocol is the same; the scanning is done through the remote techniques taught in Quantum Biomagnetism while you rest at home on a video call. Many clients alternate remote and in-person visits.
