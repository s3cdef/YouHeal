A session, step by step

Press play and watch a session unfold on Sandra's table, or step through it at your own pace. Switch to remote to see how the same session happens from home.

00:00 session clock

    What you may feel

    WarmthMany people notice gentle heat or tingling where a pair is placed.
    Deep restThe room is quiet and dim. Most clients drift toward sleep while the pairs hold.
    ReleaseA long exhale, a lighter walk out the door. Hydrate well afterward.

    Before you come

    • Wear comfortable clothes. You will not undress.
    • Empty your pockets: phone, keys, cards. Magnets and cards do not mix.
    • Drink water during the day. Eat lightly in the hour before.
    • Tell Sandra about pacemakers, implanted devices, insulin pumps or pregnancy before booking.
    • Keep taking any medication your doctor prescribes.

    After the session

    WaterTwo or three extra glasses through the evening.
    RestA slow evening helps the work settle. Skip heavy training that day.
    NoticeWrite down what changes over the next three days. It guides the next scan.
