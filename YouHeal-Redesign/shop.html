What a magnet does in this work

A therapy magnet is a static field, no electricity, no heat. In the biomagnetic pair it is always used in twos: one face of one magnet on one point, the opposite face of the other on its partner.

+

North pole (+)

Expansive · alkaline side of the pair

  • Marked red on our magnets.
  • Placed on the point the scan identifies as the alkaline, expanded side.
  • Never used alone for long periods; it belongs to a pair.
−

South pole (−)

Contractive · acidic side of the pair

  • Marked blue on our magnets.
  • Placed on the partner point, the acidic, contracted side.
  • The two faces complete the circuit of the pair.

How strong is strong enough

Strength is measured in gauss. A fridge magnet is around fifty. Therapy magnets used in the biomagnetic pair are typically one thousand gauss and above, which is still thousands of times weaker than an MRI scanner. Sandra uses the mid range for most pairs and the stronger set for posterior points, where the magnet sits under the body.

Using a pair at home

  1. Only the pairs Sandra marked

    Your session notes list the pairs and the exact points. The kit's field map helps you find them again.

  2. North on one point, south on the other

    Red face down on the first point, blue face down on its partner, over clothing. A soft band or tape holds them.

  3. Fifteen to twenty-five minutes

    Lie down, breathe, let them work. Remove both at the same time and drink water.

  4. Keep notes

    What you noticed, and when. Bring it to your next scan.

Please read before using any magnet

  • Do not use with a pacemaker, implanted defibrillator, cochlear implant, insulin pump or any implanted electronic device.
  • Do not use during pregnancy without talking to Sandra and your physician.
  • Keep magnets away from cards, phones, watches and hard drives. Keep them out of reach of children: swallowed magnets are a medical emergency.
  • Strong magnets can pinch skin when they snap together. Separate them by sliding, not pulling.
  • Home use complements your sessions and your medical care. It does not replace either.
