How the pair is explained

In Dr. Goiz's model, a dysfunction shows up as two points on the body whose local pH has drifted in opposite directions: one slightly acidic, one slightly alkaline. The two points are said to resonate with each other, and placing a magnet of the opposite polarity on each is proposed to bring both back toward neutral.

The scan reads the body's response to a magnet held at each point. When a point is active, the practitioner observes a brief change in apparent leg length, the sign Dr. Goiz adopted from earlier reports. Drag the slider to see the idea.
